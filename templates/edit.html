Formatting Guide
  • **bold** → bold
  • _italic_ → italic
  • >quote → quotation (indents text)
  • --- or ___ → draw a horizontal line
  • # Heading
  • ## Subheading
  • ### Sub-subheading
  • [link](https://example.com)
  • ![image](https://example.com/photo.jpg)
  • - item
    - item
    for a bullet list
  • 1. item
    2. item
    for a numbered list
