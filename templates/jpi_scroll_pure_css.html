Chapter I

Rhapsodie Theoter

During the Nazi occupation, John Paul co-founded a theater company called Rhpapsodic Theater. The Nazis had banned all Polish theater so they operated in secret performing in people's homes. Rhapsodic theater rejected elaborate sets and focused on the actors performances. During this time John Paul began considering the priesthood.

Chapter II

Dismantling the Iron Curtain

His 1979 return to Communist Poland ignited the moral revolution of the Solidarity movement.

Without firing a single shot, John Paul II wielded moral authority to challenge Soviet hegemony. Historians globally recognize his historic visits to Central and Eastern Europe as the catalyst that brought down the Berlin Wall.
